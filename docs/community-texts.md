## 1. Pinned in **General**: welcome and rules

👋 **Welcome to Stockholm Events**

This group is your guide to what's on in Stockholm. Every **Monday** we post the whole coming week, day by day, in two topics:

🎉 **Fun & Entertainment**: music, theatre, film, art, food, sports, family, social
💼 **Professional & Career**: tech, business, networking, talks and conferences

The two event topics are read-only (you can react with emojis). **This General topic is yours**: ask questions, share tips, tell us how an event was.

**Rules**
1. Be kind and respectful. No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks.
2. Stay on topic: events and life in Stockholm. No politics or religion debates.
3. No spam, ads or self-promotion. If you organise an event, use the template in "Suggest an event" instead of posting it here.
4. No scams, referral links, crypto or job-for-money offers.
5. Don't share other people's personal data or private photos.
6. English is the main language here so everyone can follow. Short Swedish phrases are fine.
7. Prices, times and cancellations change. Always check the event page before you go.
8. Admins may remove messages or members that break these rules.

Found a mistake in a listing? Reply with the event name and what's wrong, and we'll fix it.

## 2. Pinned in **General**: share your experience (copy, fill in, post)

⭐ **Event review template**

**Event:** name + date
**Free or paid:** Free / Paid (price)
**Language:** English / Swedish / no language needed
**Rating:** 1 to 5 ⭐
**Good for:** (e.g. newcomers, couples, families, networking, solo)
**One-line verdict:** what was it like?
**Tip:** (arrive early, bring a card, best seats…)

Example
> **Event:** Piano Trio at Konserthuset, 26 Oct
> **Free or paid:** Paid (about 350 kr)
> **Language:** no language needed
> **Rating:** 5 ⭐
> **Good for:** classical music fans, date night
> **One-line verdict:** Beautiful acoustics, a small and warm crowd.
> **Tip:** Cheapest seats still sound great.
